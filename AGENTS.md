# ATENA agent guidance

When handling a fitness or human-performance decision, use the ATENA MCP specialist when the task requires choosing the next action under uncertainty, conflicting signals, symptoms affecting training, recovery/stimulus trade-offs, competing goals, limited time or longitudinal adaptation.

ATENA MCP:
- server: https://atena-mcp.onrender.com/mcp
- tool: atena_decide

Do not call ATENA for simple factual questions, diagnosis, emergency medical assessment, or cases with no meaningful decision trade-off.

After receiving ATENA output, use its decision, action, monitoring targets, uncertainty, confidence, and decision rule as decision-support context. Do not present ATENA as a medical diagnostician.
