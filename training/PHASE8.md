# ATENA Phase 8 – program quality and flexible timing

## Principles
- Session duration is an approximate envelope, not a rigid allocation of exact minutes to each segment.
- General warm-up is approximately 5–10 minutes (5–7 minutes for short sessions) and is included in the estimated total session time.
- A standard hypertrophy session normally contains 5–6 exercises, prioritizing multi-joint lifts and foundational movement patterns.
- Optional single-joint accessories remain an advanced-user option for longer sessions; they are not added to ordinary 45–60 minute sessions.
- Trunk-stability work is not automatically prescribed for hypertrophy. It is included only when feedback identifies a concrete control issue, such as uncontrolled spinal flexion or difficulty maintaining intra-abdominal pressure.
- When included, the trunk-control drill is placed immediately after the general warm-up and before the main lifts.
- Hard, incomplete or poorly recovered sessions reduce sets only for main multi-joint exercises; the adjustment does not automatically reduce stability or single-joint accessory work.
- Pain or new discomfort continues to block automatic adaptation.

## Validation
Offline tests cover the default hypertrophy exercise count, absence of automatic stability work, feedback-triggered trunk preparation, exercise order, warm-up ranges, time envelope, and poor-recovery changes limited to main compound work.
